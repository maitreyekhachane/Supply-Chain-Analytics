# Power BI — Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains the Power BI dashboard developed for the DataCo Supply Chain Analytics project. The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and interactive visualizations to help explore sales, profitability, customer segments, markets, and delivery performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Features

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- DAX measures
- KPI cards
- Interactive charts and filters
- Time-based sales analysis
- Product and market comparisons
- Delivery performance analysis
- Tooltips and drill-through, where applicable

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Index
Dashboard navigation and project overview.

### 2. Executive Overview
- Total sales
- Total profit
- Profit margin
- Total orders
- Total customers
- Average order value

### 3. Sales & Product Performance
- Product sales and profitability
- Product and category comparisons
- Sales trends over time

### 4. Market & Customer Analysis
- Sales by market
- Customer-segment performance
- Market-level comparisons

### 5. Delivery & Operational Performance
- Delivery status distribution
- Late delivery rate
- Shipping-mode comparisons
- Shipping delay analysis

### 6. Insights & Recommendations
- Key findings
- Profitability improvement opportunities
- Delivery and operational risks
- Business recommendations

## 📈 Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately 36.78M.
- Total profit was approximately 3.97M.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 10.78%.
- Approximately 54.83% of order-item records were marked as late deliveries.
- Negative-profit records highlight areas for further investigation into pricing, costs, and discounts.

## 🎯 Outcome

The Power BI dashboard brings together the project's key metrics and analyses in an interactive format, helping users explore performance and identify areas that may require further investigation.
